# Surface brief: site one-page La Fleur d'Or (index.html)

## Scope and mode
Whole public site as a single page: hero, set menus, full carte (every dish of the printed A3 menu), practical info. Visitor mode: Persuade, with a Read/Operate core (the carte).

## Audience, job, action
People from Grenade and nearby choosing what to eat or order tonight, mostly on a phone. Job: find a dish or set menu and its price, check it is open, then call. Primary action: call 05 61 82 43 56. Secondary: browse the carte, get directions.

## Proof and content
The real menu (about 200 lines), the restaurant's Chinese name 金花餐廳, photos of the dining room, the facade and dishes from the old site, one Unsplash sushi photo. No reviews or press: none may be invented.

## Constraints
No online ordering. Weekday-lunch conditions and waiting times shown plainly. Every price shown exactly as printed.

## Direction contract
THESIS: Three cuisines under one roof, built like Grenade's brick halle: the page is a covered market whose bays hold Chinese, Thai and Japanese food, and whose carte is laid out on the bastide's checkerboard. It refuses both category defaults: red lanterns, gold dragons and cherry blossoms, and the zen-white sushi-bar minimalism.
OWN-WORLD: Toulouse brick drenching whole regions (#B5462C with darker #8E3421 courses), lime-mortar joints as every rule and leader line, slate-black market boards (#23272B) carrying chalk-white type and gold prices (#F2B53A, the golden flower), oak-beam bars (#3A2A20) for navigation and footer. One family, Archivo: condensed 800 for fascia-style display, normal-width 400 for reading, tabular numerals for every price. The brand mark is the old sign's 金花餐廳, each character set in a disc.
STORY: The visitor sees the name and three open bays of food at once, understands "Chinese, Thai, Japanese, here in Grenade", sees live whether it is open, then either calls or goes through the plan of the carte, searches a dish, jots it on the order pad and calls with the list in hand.
FIRST VIEWPORT: An oak beam bar on top: mark on the left, links in the middle, the phone number as a gold button on the right. Below it the whole field is brick. Left 55%: "La Fleur d'Or" in condensed chalk capitals (6rem), a line with the three cuisines and Grenade, a live open/closed line, then "Appeler" (gold on slate, primary) and "Voir la carte · N plats" (secondary). Right 45%: three tall photo bays between brick pillars under the beam, labelled Chine, Thaïlande, Japon; their iron shutters are up when it is open and a third of the way down, with the reopening time painted on them, when it is closed. On a phone the bays sit as a row of three narrow bays under the text, and the call button stays in a sticky bottom bar.
FORM: Grenade's brick halle and bastide grid, position 6 on the ordered list (1 takeaway flyer, 2 blue-and-white porcelain, 3 conveyor-plate colour code, 4 Asian grocery packaging, 5 Thai painted shop signs, 6 brick halle and bastide, 7 golden chrysanthemum). Seed key edbb0fbb. Signature interaction: the order pad (carnet), where + on any dish builds a running list and total to read out on the phone. Motion grammar: the bay shutters roll up once on load, with an exponential ease-out from a visible state; nothing else enters.
RAISES: from the particle event display (declined): isolate and dim; the search dims the plan's blocks that hold no match instead of reshuffling them. From the variable-font specimen (declined): hierarchy by scale contrast alone, no boxes around menu rows. From the brick instruction book (declined): one module rules every alignment, the brick course (row height) sets the carte's line grid. From the ikebana alcove (declined): one quiet, charged passage (the takeaway band) between the dense runs. From the sampler (declined): sections reflow as whole bands, never split mid-group. From the civic prospectus (declined): one face, two weights (400 and 800).
FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
